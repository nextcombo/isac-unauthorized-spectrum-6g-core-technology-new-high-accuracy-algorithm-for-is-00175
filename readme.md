The high similarity in hardware architecture, antenna structure, and spectrum bands between ISAC systems and conventional ones provides feasibility for integrated perception and communication design, making ISAC systems a research hotspot in both academia and industry.
The scarcity of spectrum resources has driven the development of cognitive radio technology, with non-authorized spectrum being widely applied in LTE-U, 5G NR-U, etc. This necessitates ISAC systems to operate in non-continuous spectrum bands. However, traditional ISAC signal processing algorithms suffer from issues such as degradation in noise resistance, resolution, and deterioration of Fourier sidelobes due to non-continuity of spectrum. Enhancing perception capability under non-continuous spectrum becomes a significant challenge.
Currently, the solutions to this challenge are mainly divided into two categories: signal design and signal processing. In terms of signal design, research has primarily focused on the design and optimization of OFDM pilot signals, but the optimization algorithm based on semidefinite relaxation performs poorly in multi-objective detection. Regarding signal processing, although existing methods have improved perception performance to some extent, there are still issues such as a reduction in maximum unambiguous distance and limited sidelobe suppression.
